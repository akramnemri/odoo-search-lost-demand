Changes of each variant (relative to its parent):

- `e01-exact-words`: `{"fuzziness": "none"}` — Typo tolerance also matches near-miss words; dev queries rarely contain typos, so exact words may rank better on dev and worse on dev-typo.
- `e02-prefix-0`: `{"prefix_length": 0}` — Letting the first letter be wrong too finds first-letter typos (the typo model makes them).
- `e03-prefix-2`: `{"prefix_length": 2}` — Two exact first letters cut many accidental near-miss matches at a small typo cost.
- `e04-most-fields`: `{"match_type": "most_fields"}` — Adding the scores of all fields rewards products whose name, brand and description all match.
- `e05-tie-breaker`: `{"tie_breaker": 0.3}` — Best field plus 30 % of the others: a middle way between best_fields and most_fields.
- `e06-cross-fields`: `{"fuzziness": "none", "match_type": "cross_fields"}` — Treating name, brand and description as one field lets a query's words match across them (no typo tolerance: Elasticsearch refuses fuzziness with cross_fields).
- `e07-most-words`: `{"minimum_should_match": "2<75%"}` — Requiring most query words (all of 1-2 words, 75 % beyond) removes the long tail of weak matches seen in Task 3.3.
- `e08-name-phrase`: `{"name_phrase_boost": 2.0}` — A bonus when the query's words appear together in the product name lifts exact products.
- `e09-less-description`: `{"fields": ["name^3", "brand^2", "description^0.5"]}` — Long descriptions mention many things; half their weight should reduce off-topic matches.
- `e10-name-exact-words`: `{"fields": ["name^3", "name.std^1", "brand^2", "description"]}` — A bonus for the name's unstemmed words (name.std) favours products that use the shopper's exact word form.
- `e11-reference`: `{"ref_exact_boost": 10.0}` — Odoo's own search finds a product by its internal reference; an exact, case-insensitive match on it should cost nothing on dev.
- `c01-combined`: `{"ref_exact_boost": 10.0, "tie_breaker": 0.3}` — The two changes kept in stage 1 together: tie-breaker 0.3 and the internal-reference match.
- `s01-boosted-1-2`: `{"stock_factor": 1.2, "stock_rule": "boosted"}` — In-stock scores x1.2: a mild preference that keeps very relevant sold-out products near the top.
- `s02-boosted-2-0`: `{"stock_factor": 2.0, "stock_rule": "boosted"}` — In-stock scores x2.0: a strong preference, close to rule (a) for most queries.
